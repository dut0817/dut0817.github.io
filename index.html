Yeji Kim

I completed my M.Sc. in Computing Science at the University of Alberta, advised by Prof. Randy Goebel and Prof. Mi-Young Kim. My research focuses on explainable AI, with a particular interest in interpretability. Beyond predictive accuracy, I study how models reach their decisions and how to make those decisions more transparent, faithful, and understandable.

Before starting my Master’s, I spent nearly five years in IT System Design at KT Corporation, one of Korea’s largest telecommunications companies. My work focused on designing workflows for location-based services and improving positioning systems.
